# What Populr Does

## The problem

Early-stage companies can't afford a marketing team. So the founder does it — badly, in
gaps between everything else. Content goes out in bursts, then stops. Nobody knows what
worked. The competition, who did hire someone, pulls ahead.

Hiring a CMO costs more than most seed-stage companies raise in a year.

## What Populr is

An AI marketing team that works for you. You give it your website. It reads your site,
works out what you sell and who you sell to, and from then on it plans campaigns, writes
the content, publishes it, watches what happened, and gets better at it.

You stay in control. Nothing goes out without your say-so unless you turn that off.

## Getting started

**Paste your website.** That's the whole setup. Populr reads it, works out your product,
your audience and your positioning, and builds your first plan. No forms, no onboarding
call, no configuration.

Everything after that is informed by what it learned. When it writes a post, it writes
about *your* product, not a generic one.

## What it does

### Writes your content

Type one sentence — *"We shipped a feature that removes the manual step our users hate"* —
and Populr writes it properly. A blog post, a LinkedIn post, an email, a landing page,
a thread, a product announcement.

It writes a **different version for each platform**. Not the same words trimmed to fit —
LinkedIn gets a LinkedIn post, X gets something that works on X. Each one is checked
against that platform's real rules before it goes anywhere.

You get hashtags, a call to action and a suggested posting time along with it.

### Publishes everywhere, in one click

Connect LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, X, Threads or Pinterest once. After that,
publishing is one button.

If a platform is briefly down or rate-limits you, Populr retries with sensible waits. If
your connection to a platform expires, it stops and tells you plainly — rather than
retrying forever against a login that can't fix itself.

### Publishes on a schedule, by itself

Tell it in a sentence:

> *"Publish 3 LinkedIn posts every week, 2 X posts daily, and an Instagram carousel every Friday."*

Populr turns that into a real schedule and keeps it. Every minute it checks what's due and
publishes it. If it runs out of things you've written, it writes new ones itself, using
everything it knows about your business.

Weekly posts are spread across different days, not fired on three consecutive ones —
because that reads like a bot, not a company.

### Plans and runs whole launches

The **Launch Workspace** takes one mission — *"Launch our AI product"* — and turns it into
a full campaign: what to make, in what order, on which channel, on which day, and which
piece depends on which other piece.

Then it runs it. You can watch each step happen, pause anything, and approve what matters.

### Works as a team of seven specialists

Behind the scenes, seven agents do the work, each with one job:

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Research** | Watches your market, competitors and trends |
| **Strategy** | Turns findings into a plan with a cadence and success metrics |
| **Content** | Writes everything, per platform |
| **Creative** | Produces the visuals and keeps them on brand |
| **Publishing** | Gets approved work out safely, everywhere |
| **Analytics** | Reports what actually happened |
| **Learning** | Makes the next campaign better than this one |

You can see what each is doing right now, why it made a choice, how confident it is, and
how long it took. You can pause any of them mid-campaign.

### Watches your market

Populr tracks what's trending in your space, what competitors are publishing, and which
search terms you could realistically win. When something changes, it tells you — and
suggests what to do about it.

It never changes a running campaign on its own. It proposes, with the evidence attached,
and you decide.

### Learns what works

Every post that goes out is measured. Populr builds a picture of what performs for *your*
audience — which formats, which angles, which times — and uses it when writing the next
thing.

This compounds. Month three is better than month one because it has seen more.

### Makes UGC video scripts

For creator-style video, Populr writes the hook, the script — with what's said *and*
what's on screen at each moment — the caption and the hashtags. Several genuinely different
versions, so you're choosing between real options rather than synonyms.

## Things we decided on purpose

**It tells you when it's guessing.** Every piece of content shows what wrote it, how
confident it was, and why it made that choice. If it had to fall back to a simpler method
because an AI provider was down, it says so rather than passing the result off as its best
work.

**It refuses rather than invents.** No connected account? It says so instead of queueing
posts that can never go out. No results to learn from yet? It says "nothing learned yet"
rather than inventing a pattern that would poison every future recommendation.

**It won't publish twice.** Whatever happens — a crash, a retry, a double-click — the same
post cannot go out twice. That's built into the foundations, not bolted on.

**Nothing goes out unapproved by default.** Approval is on when you start. You can turn it
off per campaign once you trust it.

**Warnings don't block you.** A missing alt text or a weak call to action is flagged, not
enforced. Software that refuses to publish over a judgement call is software people learn
to work around.

## What you actually do day to day

1. Open Populr. See what's scheduled, what published, what needs you.
2. Approve anything waiting.
3. Occasionally type a sentence to create something new.
4. Read what worked.

That's it. The rest runs.

## Common questions

**Do I need to know marketing?**
No. That's the point. If you can describe what you shipped, Populr can market it.

**Will it post something embarrassing?**
Not without your approval — that's on by default. Everything is also checked against each
platform's rules before it goes out, and you can read every post before it publishes.

**What if the AI is down?**
Populr keeps working with a simpler built-in writer, and labels the output so you know.
Scheduled posts still go out.

**Can I edit what it writes?**
Yes. Everything it produces is a draft until you publish it.

**What happens to my data?**
It reads your public website and the analytics accounts you connect. Platform passwords
are never stored — connections use each platform's official sign-in, and the resulting
access tokens are encrypted.

**How is this different from ChatGPT?**
ChatGPT writes text when you ask. Populr knows your business, decides what's worth doing,
writes it, publishes it on schedule, measures the result, and adjusts. The writing is the
smallest part.
